# Startup Launch Kit: Workbook

Welcome! In this workshop you will put your startup online with Cloudflare. The starter site is about a fictional startup, **Plovify** ("Plov as a Service"): replace it with yours, or keep it and tweak it. You do **not** need to know how to code. You will copy, paste and click.

By the end you will have:

- a landing page about your startup, at your own web address,
- a waitlist form that stores emails in a database,
- an AI assistant that answers your visitors' questions about your startup, in their own language.

Everything runs on the Cloudflare free plan, for free. No credit card needed.

And it is not a toy: **your prototype is your production.** The same setup can serve real customers, with no rewrite and no migration.

## Rules of the road

1. Do one step at a time. Each step is one action.
2. When a step says **"replace the whole file"**, select everything in the file (Ctrl+A on Windows, Cmd+A on Mac), delete it, then paste.
3. After each level there is a **Checkpoint**. Paste your link in the webinar chat so we know you are on track.
4. Stuck for more than 3 minutes? Write in the webinar chat, and see the **Stuck?** section at the end of each level.
